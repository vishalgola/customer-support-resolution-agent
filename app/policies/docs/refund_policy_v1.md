# Refund Policy (synthetic, for project use)

## 1. Scope
This policy covers refunds for items purchased from the store and returned by the customer. All amounts are in INR.

## 2. Return window
A return must be received by the store within 30 days of the delivery date to be eligible for a refund.

## 3. Refund eligibility
A refund becomes eligible after the return is received and the returned item passes inspection. A refund is not eligible while the return is still in transit or while inspection is pending.

## 4. Refund amount
The refund equals the amount paid for the order, minus any amount already refunded. Refunds go to the original payment method.

## 5. Approval
A support reviewer must approve every refund before it is issued.

## 6. Processing time
After approval, the payment provider normally completes a refund within 5 to 7 business days. A refund that has been accepted but not completed is shown as processing.
