# Damaged Products (synthetic)

## 1. Reporting window
Damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery. The customer must describe the damage and provide photos.

## 2. Resolution options
For a verified damaged item the store offers a replacement or a refund. A reviewer decides which option applies.

## 3. Late reports
Damage reported more than 7 days after delivery cannot be resolved automatically and must be escalated to a reviewer.
